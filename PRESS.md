## Company Boilerplate

**Short (one line)**
Liqo is Global Payments Infrastructure for Modern Businesses that lets any business accept fiat, stablecoins, or crypto and settle in the asset they want — through a single API.

**Standard (50 words)**
Liqo is Global Payments Infrastructure for Modern Businesses. It lets businesses accept payments in local fiat, stablecoins, or crypto from users anywhere and settle in their preferred currency — without holding reserves, running a treasury, or integrating providers directly. Liqo routes intelligently across liquidity partners and settles on Stellar. Learn more at https://liqo.network.

**Extended (100 words)**
Liqo is Global Payments Infrastructure for Modern Businesses that removes the financial engineering behind moving money across currencies and assets. Any business — a wallet, creator platform, marketplace, or fintech — can integrate Liqo once and then accept payments in local fiat, stablecoins, or crypto and deliver value in the asset they choose, through a single API. Liqo generates and scores routes across external liquidity and payment partners, executes the best one, and settles on the Stellar network. Starting with African and cross-border corridors (NGN, GHS, ZAR) and XLM settlement, Liqo aims to become the operating system for how businesses move money globally.

## Product Summary

- **Category:** Payments / fintech infrastructure / developer tools
- **What it does:** single-API acceptance of fiat/crypto with routed, reserve-less settlement
- **Settlement network:** Stellar (XLM, USDC)
- **Starting markets:** Africa & cross-border corridors (USD, NGN, GHS, GBP, EUR, ZAR)
- **Interfaces:** REST API, `@liqo/sdk`, hosted checkout, merchant dashboard
- **Status:** platform live in development; public waitlist open at [liqo.network/waitlist](https://liqo.network/waitlist)
